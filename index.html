01 — About

Student by day,
shipper by night.

I'm Amaar — an Artificial Intelligence student at Aror University of Art, Architecture, Design and Heritage in Sukkur, and a full-stack developer who ships complete products, not just class assignments.

My work ranges from MailForge AI, a PHP 8 email-marketing SaaS with 117+ files, 30+ database tables and 18 Groq-powered AI features, to Alexa, a Python desktop agent that takes voice commands in English and Urdu and lets me control my machine over Telegram.

I care about the parts users actually touch: fast interfaces, thoughtful motion, and layouts that hold up on a 360 pixel screen. Between classes I'm usually wiring an AI API into something useful, refactoring a Java portal, or obsessing over easing curves.
